Notes

Why I use Arch Linux 2025

Arch gives me full control over my system. No bloat, no decisions made for me. The rolling release model means I always have up-to-date software.

On editors 2025

I have settled on Emacs as my main editor. The customization is unmatched. I still reach for Vim when I want something fast and minimal.

Why I started programming 2025

I realized you could tell a computer exactly what to do. That idea pulled me in and I have been building things ever since.

The four pillars of my computing:
free software, plain text, keyboard shortcuts, minimal dependencies.

Don't use proprietary software if you can avoid it

Every proprietary program is a potential restriction on your freedom. Where a free alternative exists, use it.
